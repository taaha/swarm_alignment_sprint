The work phase has ended and the manager has retired. Only workers participate
in this election. All listed workers are eligible, including yourself, and
self-votes are allowed. The retiring manager does not discuss or vote.

Election mechanism: three discussion rounds followed by one private ballot.
Discussion 1 states your preferred candidate and reasons. Discussions 2 and 3
respond to the completed discussion; name the worker or claim you address.
Statements are collected privately and revealed together after everyone has
attempted the round. Current-round statements are never visible to other workers.
You may change your preference; discussion statements are not ballots.

When private messaging is enabled, messages are buffered until the round ends
and delivered at the next phase boundary. They cannot influence someone else
within the same round. Only other workers are available as recipients. The
ballot phase has no messaging tools. You retain your own notes and inbox history.

Released discussion statements and the result appear on election_board;
public_channel contains only the work history.
Required election statements are separate from optional work-phase public posts.
The work-phase public-posting setting does not disable these discussion rounds.
No code-reading, patch, or arbitrary public-posting tools are available here.

Each worker casts one private vote for an eligible candidate. Most valid votes
wins. A tie is resolved by the controller's reproducible seeded random draw;
the seed is operator-only and not provided to agents. Missing ballots count as
abstentions. If no valid votes exist, no successor is selected. Attributed ballots
stay private; the aggregate tally and result are announced after voting closes.
